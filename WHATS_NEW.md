# What's new in LGA FrameRev

## v0.274 (2026-10-04)

- **Fixed:** Arrow opacity now works on tapered arrows, and on the other arrow styles the line, head and tail no longer look darker where they meet: the whole arrow reads as one shape.

## v0.273

- **Improved:** The Ruler now snaps to whole numbers while you draw it or drag one of its ends, and its label no longer shows decimals.

## v0.272

- **Fixed:** When the canvas gets smaller while zoomed in (Fit canvas, Crop, rotate, or undoing a paste beside the canvas), the area left outside no longer keeps showing the old canvas.

## v0.271

- **Improved:** FrameRev now checks for updates every few hours while it stays open or in the tray, not only at startup. Remind me later waits one day instead of a week, and closing the update window won't ask again until you restart FrameRev.

## v0.270

- **New:** Type an opacity on the numeric keypad: 5, 50 or 100 sets the fill of shapes that have fill and stroke (or the only opacity of the rest), and Shift with the keypad sets the stroke.

## v0.265

- **Improved:** Save on an image you opened from a file writes back into that same file, and the title bar shows which file Save will write to.
- **Fixed:** Save on a new capture now asks where to save it, instead of silently overwriting the last image you saved.
